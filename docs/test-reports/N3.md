## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone N3 implements the explicit Concentric scene representation, annular rendering pipeline, stable-target opacity-only animation contract, conservative analytic damage tracking, zero-allocation warm rendering cache, and deterministic visual sandbox export pack specified in `INTERACTION_DESIGN.md` (§I8), `ARCHITECTURE.md` (§A4), `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` (§N3), and `TEST_MATRIX.md` (`T-REN1`–`T-REN7`, `T-NAV10`–`T-NAV12`, `T-REG1`–`T-REG2`, `T-PERF1`–`T-PERF3`).

In accordance with the owner's authorization and `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §N3:
- `crates/radivine-wheel` remains `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, pure Rust, platform-independent, renderer-independent, and depends solely on `smallvec` at runtime.
- `crates/radivine-render` remains `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` and platform-independent.
- No new font or icon packages were added, and all protected branding and icon assets under `assets/branding/` and `assets/icons/` remain untouched.
- The production desktop safety guard in `apps/radivine/src/session.rs` remains active (clamping `WheelLayout::Concentric` to `WheelLayout::Branching` until N4), and `WheelLayout::Concentric` is not yet exposed in `apps/radivine-control`.
- No installed Radivine `v0.2.0` daemon, user configuration, systemd service, or Nemo integration was started, stopped, or modified.

## 2. Summary of N3 Functionality & API Extensions

### 2.1 Explicit Scene Representation (`crates/radivine-wheel/src/scene.rs`)
- **`WheelVisualKind`:** Explicitly distinguishes `Branching`, `ConcentricRoot`, and `ConcentricRing` (`#[derive(..., Default)]` with `#[default] Branching`). The renderer dispatches strictly on `WheelVisualKind` and never infers layout from radii, bridge absence, or wheel index.
- **`SelectionVisual`:** Strongly typed segment selection state (`None`, `Pending`, `Armed`, `KeyboardFocus`) on `SegmentVisual.selection`.
- **`NestedSegmentContent` & `ContentMode`:** Re-exported from `radivine_wheel` (`rect: Rect`, `mode: ContentMode::{IconAndLabel, IconOnly, LabelOnly}`) and attached to `WheelVisual.nested_content: Option<SmallVec<[NestedSegmentContent; 12]>>` alongside `WheelVisual.separator_half_angle: Option<f32>`.
- **`OriginGuideVisual`:** Added `Scene.origin_guide: Option<OriginGuideVisual>` (`anchor`, `center`, `opacity`) emitted when a Concentric wheel center is displaced `> 8 lp` from the activation anchor.
- **`CaptionVisual.breadcrumb`:** Added `breadcrumb: Option<SmallVec<[Arc<str>; 6]>>` carrying the open ancestor labels for Concentric captions while keeping `CaptionVisual.title` for the primary item/submenu label and `CaptionVisual.detail` for description, disabled reason, or `"Move inward to go back."`.

### 2.2 Stable-Target Concentric Animation (`crates/radivine-wheel/src/{concentric,session}.rs`)
- **Fixed Geometry:** All Concentric rings keep `scale = 1.0`, `origin = center`, and segment `pop = 0.0` throughout appearance, open navigation, sibling replacement, and dismissal.
- **Opacity-Only Transitions:**
  - Active ring target opacity `1.0`; immediate parent `0.78`; older ancestors `0.62` (minimum `0.55`).
  - Ring appearance: `90 ms` `EaseOutCubic` (`45 ms` in `reduced_motion`).
  - Ring retraction: `80 ms` `EaseOutCubic` (`45 ms` in `reduced_motion`).
  - Segment highlight tween: `70 ms` (`35 ms` in `reduced_motion`).
  - Interrupted animations retarget continuously from current interpolated opacity without jumps.
  - Retracting rings live solely in visual `retracting_rings` (bounded `<= 1` per level slot) and are immediately excluded from `open_rings` hit testing.

### 2.3 Concentric Rendering & Asset Caching (`crates/radivine-render/src/{lib,theme,cache}.rs`)
- **Theme Extension (`theme.rs`):** Added `Theme.nested_outline: ColorRgba` (`#E8F5F0` in dark theme, `#073B30` in light theme) for the `2 lp` inner contrast outline on `SelectionVisual::{Armed, KeyboardFocus}` sectors.
- **Annular Rendering (`lib.rs`):**
  - `OriginGuideVisual` renders below all ring material (`1.5 lp` line + `3 lp` anchor dot at `0.35 * root.opacity`).
  - `WheelVisualKind::ConcentricRoot` renders the level-0 annular sectors and the single shared central hub (`r = 40 lp` or `56 lp`), with no blurred full-disc drop shadow.
  - `WheelVisualKind::ConcentricRing` renders annular sector material, `separator_half_angle` radial gaps, outer-rim submenu markers, selection outlines, and sector content inside the N2-supplied `NestedSegmentContent.rect`, with **no child hub**, **no full-disc shadow**, and **no stems**.
  - Single-item rings (`n == 1`) render as a continuous `2π` annulus (`FillRule::EvenOdd`) with no self-separator seam.
  - Concentric captions (`280 × 54 lp`) compose cached per-crumb text masks (`AssetCache::layout_breadcrumb`) with `" › "` separators and oldest-middle-first `"…"` ellipsis truncation, 1-line bold title (`13 lp`), and up to 2-line detail/disabled reason (`11 lp`).
- **Zero-Allocation Warm Path (`cache.rs`, `lib.rs`):**
  - Session-bounded caches for Concentric sector masks (`fill`, `stroke`, `outline`, `marker_fill`, `marker_stroke`), hub masks, badge circles, caption pills, and origin guides eliminate per-frame heap allocations on warm Concentric frames.
  - `AlphaMask::new` precomputes contiguous non-zero horizontal spans (`MaskSpan { x0, x1 }`) per row, and `Renderer::render` partitions the `<= 4` damage rectangles into mutually disjoint clip rectangles (`subtract_rect_px`), skipping `tiny_skia::Mask` fills on Concentric scenes.

### 2.4 Damage Tracking (`crates/radivine-render/src/damage.rs`)
- `hash_origin_guide`, `hash_wheel_base`, `hash_segment`, and `hash_caption` incorporate every N3 visual field (`WheelVisualKind`, `WheelRole`, `separator_half_angle`, `NestedSegmentContent.{rect, mode}`, `SegmentVisual.selection`, `CaptionVisual.breadcrumb`, and `Scene.origin_guide`).
- `segment_bbox_px_concentric` computes exact analytic bounds over `seg.a0`, `seg.a1`, midpoint angle, and all cardinal angles `k * (π / 2)` inside `[seg.a0, seg.a1]` at both `r_in` and `r_out`, unioned with `NestedSegmentContent.rect`, and treats `n == 1` as a full disc.
- `merge_into_at_most_4` preserves separate bounding boxes for L-shaped adjacent outer ring sectors when unioning them would add `> 20%` dead area across the untouched inner rings, while still guaranteeing `<= 4` output damage rectangles.
