### 1.2 Mandatory Pre-C1 Verification Spikes `P1`–`P9` (`8e146d1`)
- Implemented and executed the isolated verification spike suite in [`spikes/convert-spikes`](../../spikes/convert-spikes/src/main.rs) (`--release`) and recorded empirical measurements in [`docs/planning/v0.2.0/spikes/C1-spikes.md`](../planning/v0.2.0/spikes/C1-spikes.md):
  - **`P1` (`libwebp` Lossy FFI & Progress Cancellation):** Verified `libwebp-sys 0.14.4` bundles `libwebp v1.6.0` (`0x010600 >= 1.3.2`) and `WebPPicture::progress_hook` aborts cleanly mid-encode (`VP8_ENC_ERROR_USER_ABORT = 10`), resolving `Q-D`.
  - **`P2` (RIFF/VP8X Metadata Muxing):** Verified pure-Rust RIFF/VP8X chunk muxer (`ICCP` + `EXIF` with 24-bit `canvas_width - 1` / `canvas_height - 1` and even-byte padding) preserves ICC and EXIF payloads byte-for-byte.
  - **`P3` (`jpeg-encoder` vs `image` JPEG Encoder):** Verified `jpeg-encoder 0.7.1` (`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, 0 transitive dependencies) is `2.6×` faster (`18.17 ms` vs `47.55 ms` on 4 MP) and produces `36%` smaller output at `q=82` (`4:2:0` + optimized Huffman coding) than `image::codecs::jpeg::JpegEncoder`, while supporting `4:4:4`/`4:2:2`/`4:2:0`, progressive DCT, `add_icc_profile`, and `add_exif_metadata`.
  - **`P4` (EXIF Orientation `1..=8` & In-Place Tag Reset):** Verified `apply_orientation` + `exif::reset_orientation_in_place` across all 8 EXIF orientations (both little-endian `II*\0` and big-endian `MM\0*`).
  - **`P5` (ICC Round-Trip Across All 9 Pairs):** Verified SHA-256 byte-exact ICC preservation across all 9 `(PNG, JPEG, WebP) × (PNG, JPEG, WebP)` pairs.
  - **`P6` (Animated WebP/APNG Detection & Bomb Rejection):** Verified animated WebP (`VP8X` bit 1 / `ANIM`) and APNG (`acTL`) return `UnsupportedFeature::Animation`, and `65535×65535` bomb headers are rejected before pixel buffer allocation (`LimitExceeded`).
  - **`P7` (Peak RSS vs `2.2 × decoded_rgba + 16 MiB` Governor Formula):** Verified that setting `picture.use_argb = 0` for lossy WebP and dropping the owned RGB/RGBA buffer immediately after `WebPPictureImport*` keeps peak `VmHWM` strictly under `2.2 × decoded_rgba + 16 MiB` across 12 MP (`99.2 MiB` vs `116.7 MiB`), 24 MP (`194.4 MiB` vs `217.4 MiB`), 50 MP (`400.7 MiB` vs `435.7 MiB`), and 100 MP (`795.3 MiB` vs `855.2 MiB`).
  - **`P8` (Two-Rung Atomic Commit Ladder):** Recorded the Linux errno classification table for Rung A (`renameat2(RENAME_NOREPLACE)`) and Rung B (`linkat` + `fsync(dest_dir)` + `unlinkat` + `fsync(stage_dir)`).
  - **`P9` (16-Bit & Grayscale/Palette PNG Handling):** Verified 16-bit PNGs preserve 16-bit precision on `PNG -> PNG` and convert cleanly to 8-bit `RGB8`/`RGBA8` for JPEG and WebP.

### 1.3 Codec-Free Conversion Core (`crates/radivine-convert`, `09b84f8`)
- Implemented [`crates/radivine-convert`](../../crates/radivine-convert/src/lib.rs) (`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, `clippy.toml` disallowing `std::fs::rename` so all commits go through the two-rung atomic ladder):
  - `format.rs`: `FormatId` (`Png`, `Jpeg`, `WebP`), `MediaKind`, `FormatTraits`, `FormatDescriptor`, `sniff_format`, `format_from_extension`, `format_from_mime`.
  - `backend.rs`: `BackendId`, `BackendKind`, `FeatureSet`, `ConversionCapability`, `ProbeSpec`, `BackendDescriptor`, `Availability`, `probe_all` (with `ExternalBackendPolicy::Disabled` in v0.2.0), `CapabilityCatalog`, deterministic routing (`priority` then lexical `BackendId`), and `NoRoute`.
  - `options.rs` & `settings.rs`: `QualityIntent`, `FormatQuality`, `QualitySetting`, `ResizeSpec`, `AlphaPolicy`, `MetadataPolicy` (`OrientationPolicy`, `IccPolicy`, `ExifPolicy`), `OutputLocation`, `CollisionPolicy`, `SameFormatPolicy`, `ConversionOptions::validate`, 4 built-in presets (`web-optimized`, `balanced-photo`, `smaller-size`, `lossless-archive`), 3-layer option resolution (`defaults <- preset <- overrides`), and `ConvertSettings` lenient runtime load + comment-preserving `toml_edit` save.
  - `output.rs`: Private per-job staging directory `.rvstage-<job>-<rand16>` (`0700` + `fstat` ownership/mode verification), canonical `item-<idx>-<rand16>.part` opened with `O_CREAT | O_EXCL | O_RDWR | O_NOFOLLOW | O_CLOEXEC`, `sync_and_rewind_for_validation` (`T-VALIDATE-RDWR`), `CommitPermit<'a>`, two-rung atomic commit ladder (`Rung A: RENAME_NOREPLACE` -> `fsync(stage_dir)` -> `fsync(dest_dir)`; `Rung B: linkat` -> `fsync(dest_dir)` -> `unlinkat` -> `fsync(stage_dir)`), and `(dev, ino)`-verified `cleanup_temp_safely` + `cleanup_staging_dir_if_empty`.
  - `engine.rs` & `error.rs`: `ConversionEngine` (`plan`, `inspect`, `convert_item`), `ItemControl` (dyn-compatible `&mut dyn ItemControl` with lifetime-polymorphic `acquire_commit_permit<'a>`), pre-commit source `FileIdentity` (`dev`, `ino`, `size`, `mtime`) verification, and typed `ConversionError` / `ErrorClass`.

### 1.4 Built-In Image Backend & Audited `libwebp` Encoder Wrapper (`15828e9`)
- Implemented [`ffi/radivine-webp`](../../ffi/radivine-webp/src/lib.rs): minimal (~300 LoC) encode-only wrapper over `libwebp-sys 0.14.4` (`libwebp 1.6.0`), with `PictureGuard` RAII (`WebPPictureFree`), `picture.use_argb = 0` direct YUV420 import + immediate pixel buffer drop, and `progress_hook` mid-encode cancellation.
- Implemented [`crates/radivine-convert-image`](../../crates/radivine-convert-image/src/lib.rs) (`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`):
  - Header-only `inspect` with pre-allocation `IHDR` / `SOF` dimension checks, APNG `acTL` / WebP `VP8X` animation detection, 12-bit / arithmetic-coded JPEG detection, ICC detection, and EXIF orientation parsing.
  - Memory-safe decode with `image::Limits` (`max_image_width`, `max_image_height`, `max_alloc`), EXIF `1..=8` auto-orientation + in-place tag reset to `1`, `KeepSafe` GPS/CameraOwner/SerialNumber tag stripping, translucent alpha flattening onto `AlphaPolicy.background`, Lanczos3 resize with no-upscale default, and PNG / JPEG (`jpeg-encoder`) / lossless WebP / lossy WebP (`radivine-webp` + `riff::mux_webp_metadata`) encoding.

### 1.5 Synthetic Corpus Generator, Developer CLI & Fuzz Targets (`d1e531c`, `edf5fd1`)
- Implemented [`tools/convert-corpus`](../../tools/convert-corpus/src/lib.rs) generating deterministic synthetic sets (`basic`, `photo`, `meta`, `edge`, `malformed`, `batch`, `fs`) plus committed malformed seed fixtures (`64 KiB` total, `< 1 MB`) under `crates/radivine-convert-image/tests/fixtures/malformed/`.
- Implemented [`tools/convert-cli`](../../tools/convert-cli/src/main.rs) supporting `convert-cli`, `convert-cli inspect`, and `convert-cli bench` (with isolated child-process `VmHWM` sampling).
- Added 6 C1 fuzz targets in [`fuzz/`](../../fuzz/Cargo.toml): `sniff_format`, `inspect`, `convert_small`, `exif_orientation_reset`, `webp_riff_mux`, and `convert_toml_parse`.
